Questions

What is the best Arc Search alternative for iPhone?

Glint is built to replace Arc Search on iPhone: search-first launch, Search for Me answer pages with sources, page summaries, ad blocking and auto-archiving tabs. The AI runs on the iPhone, so it can't be switched off by a shutdown.

What happens to Arc Search on November 23, 2026?

Arc Search loses Browse for Me and its other AI features, plus sign-in and tab sync. On iPhone it keeps working as a basic browser. Dia, the same company's new browser, has no public iPhone app.

Is there a subscription?

No. Glint is free with 5 AI answers a day. Glint Pro is $4.99 once for unlimited answers and summaries.

Which iPhones get AI answers?

iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer) on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other iPhones get the browser, ad blocking and the list of sources.

Is it private?

Yes. No account, no tracking, and your questions never go to an AI server. Tabs and settings stay on your iPhone.
