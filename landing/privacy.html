Glint Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Glint does not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking.

AI answers are made on your iPhone. Search for Me and page summaries use Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Your questions and the pages Glint reads are not sent to us or to any AI service.

Searching and browsing. Like any browser, Glint sends your searches to the search engine you choose and loads the web pages you visit, which may set cookies under their own policies. Search for Me fetches results from DuckDuckGo and loads the top pages directly from your iPhone.

On your device only. Open tabs, archived tabs, settings and your daily usage count are stored on your iPhone and never leave it.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
