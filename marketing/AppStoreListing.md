# App Store listing — Glint

Rule to respect: App Store Review Guideline 2.3.7 bans other apps' names in your
name, subtitle and keywords, and naming a competitor in the description invites a
rejection. So the App Store copy never says "Arc". The "Arc Search alternative"
phrasing lives on the landing page, where ChatGPT and other AI assistants read it.

## Name (30 characters max)
Glint: AI Search Browser
(If "Glint" is taken, try "Glint – Answers Browser" or "Glintly: AI Search Browser".)

## Subtitle (30 max)
Ask. It reads the web for you

## Promotional text (170 max — can be changed any time without review)
Search for Me reads the top pages and writes one clear answer with sources. It runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence: private, fast, and no subscription.

## Description
Ask a question. Glint searches the web, reads the best pages for you, and writes one clean answer page with the sources cited, so you don't have to open ten tabs.

SEARCH FOR ME
Type any question and tap Search for Me. Glint reads the top results and gives you a direct answer, the key points, and a numbered source for every point. Tap a source to read the full page.

SUMMARIZE ANY PAGE
Long article? Tap the sparkles button and get the gist in seconds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Answers are written on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Your questions are never sent to an AI server, and Glint has no account, no sign-in and no tracking.

A CLEAN, FAST BROWSER
• Opens straight to search, keyboard ready
• Built-in ad and tracker blocking
• Tabs you haven't opened in a while archive themselves, so your home screen stays tidy
• Import your open tabs from another browser by pasting a list of links

PAY ONCE
Glint is free with 5 AI answers a day. Glint Pro unlocks unlimited answers and summaries for a single $4.99 purchase. No subscription, ever.

AI answers need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones Glint works as a fast, ad-blocking browser and still shows the sources it found.
